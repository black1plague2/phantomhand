## Open issues / decisions needing Opus or a human
1. **"Hold BOOT at reset" cannot work**: BOOT is GPIO0; held while EN is released it enters the ROM downloader and the sketch never runs. I implemented a 2 s window after reset in which pressing BOOT starts the self-test (reads GPIO0 as an input with pull-up, never drives it; this is the one deliberate use of a boot-strap pin, read-only). Serial `selftest` is the primary path. 05-ELECTRONICS-TEAM.md §6.4 says "hold BOOT at reset": needs the wording fixed.
2. **Uncompiled.** Likely risk spots for the first compile: `Adafruit_SSD1306` 6-argument constructor with `clkAfter 400000`; ArduinoJson v7 must be installed (v6 will fail on `JsonDocument` / `.to<JsonObject>()`); `oled.begin()` re-calls `Wire.begin()` (should log a harmless warning in core 3.x).
3. **OLED flush ~25 ms** (1 KB at 400 kHz) is a blocking call. Mitigated: only on change, <= 5 Hz, deferred up to 1 s while a motor is on. A stroke arriving mid-flush is delayed <= ~25 ms (inside the 100 ms touch-to-sight budget). Measure on the bench.
4. **Line 3 width**: size-2 font is 12 px/char, so only 10 chars fit in 128 px; 11-12 char texts are drawn at size 1. Text beyond 12 chars is truncated, non-ASCII dropped.
5. **MPU DLPF changed 21 Hz -> 44 Hz** (matched to 100 Hz sampling; my judgement, not in the prompt). Revert in `setup()` if the team disagrees.
6. **Subscriber policy**: implicit refreshes (commands/pings) never evict; an explicit `subscribe` evicts the least recently seen entry when full, so a laptop plot can always join. A tester that sends commands from a new ephemeral port each time can fill the table for up to 5 s. Spec says max 3; behaviour beyond that is mine.
7. `protocol_version` in discovery is still "1.0"; contracts v0.2 may want it bumped (see request below). Status also gained `device_kind`, `oled_available`, `unknown_msgs`.
8. MOTOR_0_LOCATION stays "upper_arm" / motor 1 "forearm" (schema vocabulary); in this build A is near the wrist and B toward the elbow, both on the forearm.
9. No Python/sim tests exist for this firmware; `sim/haptic` fake does not implement subscribe/display/MAX 150 (not my lane, S track).
