Gate G2, "L3 table" of `docs/agent-briefs/ph/04-E2E.md`. What ran, with the real output, and what did not.

## What "L3 with Unity" means here

`run_pipeline.py --game phantom_hand --sim` (without `--no-unity`) starts Unity in batch mode, which needs the editor closed. The
editor stays open on this PC (owner's rule: one open editor, driven through the bridge), so the Unity leg was run in two steps:

1. **Unity live against the hub and the twin**: PlayMode test `PH_FullRun_DemoMode_AgainstFakeHubAndTwin` in the open editor
   (the real PhantomHand scene, scripted participant, `sim/live/fake_hub.py` on 8787, `sim/sleeve/twin.py --kind both` on offset ports).
2. **That run's recording through the L3 table**: `run_pipeline.py --game phantom_hand --sim --no-unity --fixture <the Unity
   session folder>`. The fake headset replays the recorded events through the live protocol to a fresh hub and drives a fresh
   twin with the recorded stroke datagrams; `contracts/validate.py --session`, analytics and `tools/demo/l3_checks.py` then
   run on what the hub stored.

So the phase order, the SYNC timing and the ASYNC delay rows are Unity's own stamps; the ack, RTT, upload and analytics rows of
step 2 are measured on the replay, and Unity's own figures for them come from step 1. Unity in batch mode was not run.

## What it took to get here (all committed in 3fefd61 and 2629398)

The first replay of a Unity recording (13:23, session `d170f66d`) gave 4/7. None of the three red rows was a game defect:

- Phases: the check wanted one ProbePre and a `done` phase_start; the game probes before every condition and ends with
  `block_end` / `session_end` (03-SPEC D21). The check now accepts both shapes.
- ASYNC delay: the check compared raw send stamps with 500-700 ms; every cue leaves `tactile_lead_ms` early (D22).
- Acks: that replay ran while the Quest APK was building; the PC was overloaded and throttled.

Analytics on the same recording showed the ownership rating as `degraded` in every demo run (D23, fixed), and the game's
flinch path had never been driven (flinch check added to `PH_FullRun`).
