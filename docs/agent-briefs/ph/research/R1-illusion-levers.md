# R1 - Illusion levers for the Phantom Hand demo (research note, 8 Oct 2026)

Time-boxed on 8 Oct: nothing was opened after the S26 list in F. Tags: [EVIDENCE] a cited source says it; [INFERENCE] my reasoning from sources or from the code as read on 8 Oct (before D16 landed, so re-check line numbers); [GUESS] unverified. Source keys S1-S26 are in F; unfinished checks are in G.
Not re-argued (decided elsewhere): brush 12 cm/s with one tap per motor (R2, D16), delayed-first (D9), dissolve + fallback reveal (D13), agency opt-in (D14), lead with flinch and rating with drift weak at n = 1 (R4). Where the evidence disagrees with one of them, C says so in two lines.

## A. Summary (ten lines)
1. The build already sits inside published ranges on the big levers: first-person view, passive synchronous touch, 15 cm medial offset (rating held at roughly 14-42 cm hand gap, drift contrast only near 14 cm, ownership falls past ~27 cm), delay >= 600 ms, impact-type threat. [EVIDENCE S1, S2, S16, S17, S18]
2. The best-replicated outcome is the ownership rating, and demo mode cannot show it per person: it asks once after the last block, so one side of the witness "ownership" row is empty. Fix first. [EVIDENCE S1, S6, S7, S17; INFERENCE from code]
3. The delayed block can leak: with the A/B swap kept, a swapped B tap lands 133-333 ms BEFORE the brush reaches B (motor gap 833 ms at 12 cm/s), inside the +-300 ms zone where VR ownership was not significantly reduced. Switch the swap off. [INFERENCE; EVIDENCE S2]
4. 45 s sits on the rising limb: the sync-minus-delayed rating gap is moderate at 31-60 s and strong at 61-120 s. Use 60 s for the demo and 90 s for the pilot. [EVIDENCE S3]
5. The flinch needs three fixes before it can carry a claim: baseline before the telegraph, a softer thud, block order logged. [INFERENCE; EVIDENCE S18, S19, S26]
6. Drift is weak (null at n = 45 and n = 49, dissociates from ownership), consistent with R4: keep it as a side number. [EVIDENCE S10, S7, S6, S4]
7. Timing: the RHI tolerates +-300 ms, but VR haptic-visual simultaneity is stricter (a lag under 50 ms goes unnoticed, a lead over ~15 ms does not), so never let the buzz lead. [EVIDENCE S2, S11]
8. Cheap visual gains: cover the elbow stump, offer three skin tones, keep the realistic hand; do not overlay the arms or shrink the offset. [EVIDENCE S12, S13, S10, S14, S15]
9. Say out loud: no two-sided p < 0.05 from a rank test at n = 5, about a third of people may not feel it, ratings carry expectation, and the PRD's "2 cm" rests on 8 men. [INFERENCE; EVIDENCE S24, S22, S9]
10. Ten of the twelve changes take <= 1 h (about 7 h in all), two are M (about 5 h); no new hardware. [GUESS on hours]

## C. Do NOT do, and checks against decisions already taken
- No motor pulse at stone impact: pulses inside the response window are excluded by FR-AN-01 and add a startle of their own. [INFERENCE] (S18 paired impacts with palm vibration but scored skin conductance, not EMG.)
- No knife, needle or blood: skin conductance to a knife did not depend on synchrony (S18), and the optics are poor. [EVIDENCE]
- Do not overlay the virtual arm on the real arm or shrink the offset under ~10 cm: a realistic body in the real body's place gave ownership without synchrony in full-body VR (S15, abstract), which would turn the delayed block into a non-control. Do not pass ~25 cm: ownership fell beyond ~27 cm (S16). Keep 15 cm; side of the midline mattered little (S17). [EVIDENCE/INFERENCE]
- Do not probe drift during induction (S6), and finish the post-probe within ~40 s of the last stroke (S5). [EVIDENCE]
- Do not let any delayed tap fall within +-300 ms of a brush pass at the same spot (S2), and do not say "exactly 600 ms": the build is about 0.6 s with +-100 ms jitter (and scrambled order if the swap stays). [EVIDENCE/INFERENCE]
- Do not build the live "Ownership Index" bar from z-scored drift, flinch and q1 (additions note #7a): one person has no reference distribution, drift is weak (R4), and "assembling the Self" overclaims. [INFERENCE]
- Do not read a flat q4 as evidence: it is not a control item and expectation alone reproduces rating patterns (S22). [EVIDENCE]
- No music or sound bed over induction and probes until E-tested. [GUESS]
- R2/D16 (12 cm/s, one tap per motor): supported for ratings, since sync beat delayed at 3 cm/s but not at 18 cm/s with real touch (S7, n = 49). Contradiction: at 12 cm/s the motor gap is 833 ms, which makes the kept swap a near-sync leak (change 2). [EVIDENCE/INFERENCE]
- D9 delayed-first: no contradiction (S5 carry-over; habituation works against us, S26). Only add the order log (change 5). D13 and D14: nothing found for or against in this time box; S21 separates ownership from agency, consistent with the opt-in (title and table only). [EVIDENCE]
- R4 (lead with flinch and rating; drift weak): the drift half is supported (S6-S10). Caution on the flinch half: evidence that an EMG flinch tracks ownership is one abstract-level study (S20) and defensive responses split (S18 yes for impact, S19 no illusion interaction), so lead the claim with the rating and let the flinch lead the spectacle until changes 3-5 are in. [EVIDENCE/INFERENCE]

## D. Say out loud (n = 5) [safe vs unsafe]
- Safe: "k of 5 pilot people rated ownership higher after synchronous touch", with the raw values. Exact sign test: 5 of 5 gives one-sided p = 1/32 = 0.031 (two-sided 0.0625); 4 of 5 gives 0.19. [INFERENCE, arithmetic]
- Safe: published lab studies report higher ownership ratings after synchronous than delayed touch (S1, S6, S7, S17), and our offset and delay follow published ranges (S1, S2, S16, S17). [EVIDENCE]
- Safe: our logged touch-to-sight error (p95) and that EMG, IMU and wrist speed are captured for 1.5 s after impact. [EVIDENCE: our own logs]
- Unsafe: any p < 0.05 from a rank test at n = 5 (a Wilcoxon signed-rank test cannot go below two-sided 0.0625 at n = 5, 0.031 at n = 6). [INFERENCE, arithmetic]
- Unsafe: "drift of about 2 cm" or "drift shows ownership": 8 men in the PRD's anchor (S9), null at n = 45 and 49 (S10, S7), dissociation (S6, S4). [EVIDENCE]
- Unsafe: "the flinch is bigger because the hand felt owned": startle, habituation (S26), block order, anticipatory baseline and electrode gain all compete; compare within a session and quote the order. [INFERENCE]
- Unsafe: "a flat q4 shows awareness is unchanged" (expectation; not a control item, S22). "Everyone feels it in a minute": about two thirds of 30 able-bodied people felt ownership (S24) and 45 s shows about half the plateau gap (S3). [EVIDENCE]
- Booth tally (one-sided sign test, call it exploratory, convenience sample): 9 of 10 visitors in the predicted direction gives p = 0.011 (8 of 10: 0.055); 15 of 20 gives 0.021 (14 of 20: 0.058). [INFERENCE, arithmetic]

## E. Bench questions (about 5 minutes each)
- E1 Swap leak (with R2 E4): 2 teammates; P1 delayed block 30 s swap on, 30 s swap off; left-hand pinch whenever a touch seems to belong to the brush; keep the swap off if "on" gives more pinches.
- E2 Sound floor: 1 teammate with EMG; stone lands 30 cm beside the virtual hand at thud 1.0, 0.5, 0.25 (random, 30 s apart), then once on the hand; if the beside-hand response at 0.5 is at least half the on-hand response, lower again.
- E3 Early or late buzz (with R2 E6): 3 teammates; `tactile_lead_ms` 0, 20, 40, 60 for 10 s each, random; "buzz early, together or late?"; take the smallest lead never called early.
- E4 Onset: 3 teammates; 60 s synchronous; left-hand pinch when the arm starts to feel like theirs, or never; median onset above 25 s means keep 60 s; no pinch by 60 s marks a probable non-responder.
- E5 Probe noise floor: 1 teammate; five pointings in a row before any induction; SD above 1.5 cm means a 1-2 cm drift cannot be resolved per person (keep drift exploratory).
- E6 Habituation: 3-5 teammates; two stones 60 s apart in the same synchronous setup; second-over-first ratio of EMG peak and wrist peak; below 0.7 means order dominates the flinch contrast (quote it with the order only).
