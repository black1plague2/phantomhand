## A. Blunt assessment (ten lines)
1. **Strength.** One sentence explains it (sight and touch together make a hand "yours"; 600 ms apart largely breaks it), it has its own control, a live body signal and a Theme-5 story: the textbook design [E:S7,S8,S9]. The stone is the right threat: impact responses track ownership, knife-type threats do not (n=18) [E:S5].
2. **Biggest weakness is delivery, not science.** [R:WORKLIST] On 8 Oct the committed PhantomHand scene had no in-headset UI, session runner or scene controller wired; U4 panel tests 0/12, EditMode 329/343, PlayMode 25/29 (mostly cheap bugs: `??` on Unity objects, missing venvs); no Bootstrap scene (an APK built now would boot Orchard); U6 not started; model wrappers not baked; the electronics Wi-Fi/UDP test not done [R:PH_STATUS]; the Unity MCP also refused to connect in this review session (ECONNREFUSED). A clean 4-minute hardware run by the 12:00 freeze is plausible, not safe: plan a ladder (C1).
3. **It does not fit the slot.** 4:40 of run plus consent, sleeve, electrodes, calibration and about eight VR pokes, on a stranger. Designed public embodiment sessions run 10-20 min [E:S17,S18]. [I] Use a pre-fitted volunteer for the judged 3 minutes and a 60-s rapid mode for judges.
4. **Drift is the weakest headline.** Closest vibrotactile RHI (n=20): SYNC-ASYNC drift 0.9 ± 0.4 cm, not significant, while ratings and skin conductance were [E:S2]; drift also appears in controls [E:S3]; Quest 2 fingertip error ≈1.1 cm [E:S12]. The PRD's "≈2 cm" is glove drift versus baseline, not a contrast [E:S1]; the UI test fixture (+3.6 vs +0.4 cm, gap 3.2 cm) is 2.5-3.5x the published 0.9-1.3 cm gap [R:UiModelTests.cs:151-155], so do not tune the game to reproduce it. [I] At n=1 the sign can flip on stage, and G3's "≥4 of 5 show SYNC>ASYNC drift" is met only 34-53% of the time even if the effect is real (assuming a 60-70% chance per person of the right sign). Lead with rating + flinch.
5. **The flinch is the best theatre and the least validated measure.** The classic threat marker is skin conductance [E:S6]; forearm EMG/IMU is a proxy. [I] Startle, anticipation and order confound one trial per condition, and ASYNC-first makes stone 2 the expected one (I found no clean human-limb habituation source: unproven either way). The sleeve's own motor noise will look like flinches on a raw trace (PRD §13).
6. **Bystanders cannot decode a headset.** In a physical public RHI the mechanism is watchable (two brushes, a curtain) [E:S20]; with VR plus vibration nothing is. The second screen must show the mechanism and the quiet-stone-spike story at a glance; `live_plot.py --fullscreen` exists [R:live_plot.py] but needs gating (row 1 of B).
7. **q4 "awareness stays flat" is theatre.** A leading, ceiling-prone item built "not to move" [R:Questionnaire.cs]; a sceptic reads it as an instrument designed to confirm the thesis, and ownership questionnaires are partly suggestion-driven (disputed) [E:S4]. Keep it as a spoken closing question, not a plotted result. [O]
8. **Too many layers.** Voice-over, breath, agency, self-touch and an Ownership Index each add failure surface without new evidence; only dissolve + reveal earns its minutes, and only after G2. [O]
9. **The clinical story is lineage, not a claim.** Mirror therapy: moderate-quality motor benefit after stroke [E:S26]; VR: modest as an add-on [E:S27]; phantom-pain trials small or uncontrolled [E:S23-S25]; the RHI is a fallible proxy for prosthesis embodiment [E:S22]. Say "measurement and demonstration rig".
10. **Net.** Keep sleeve + stone + second screen; cut the philosophy layers; spend the remaining hours on the audience screen, a sleeve-only fallback station, a 60-s rapid mode and a recorded golden run. [O]

### A2. What comparable demos did, and what is established
- **Physical RHI at public events** [E:S20, snippets only]: Southampton 2014 drop-in day (a curtain hides the real hand, a paintbrush strokes both hands at once); Exploration Place (one consenting volunteer inside a live show); an NTT 2015 booth (staff explained the effect); Indiana ScienceFest (outdoors, side activities filled the slow setup). [I] The mechanism is visible, so bystanders get it in seconds.
- **SIGGRAPH Emerging Tech**: The Malleable-Self Experience (VR + whole-body haptics, 2024 Audience Award) and PinocchioVR (HMD + nose pull, 2019) [E:S19]. [I] Only the wearer sees the effect; winners lean on the wearer's reaction, hence your second screen. **Machine to Be Another**: human facilitators and protocols, ~10-15 min sessions, plus an unattended museum version [E:S17, snippets].
- **Haptic sleeves**: bHaptics TactSleeve = 3 ERM motors per arm, BLE, Quest-compatible [E:S37]; Tactile Brush: widely spaced motors can feel like one moving stroke [E:S36]. Hardware is not your novelty; timing + readout are [I]. Two motors as a brush: see R2.
- **Established**: synchrony-dependent ownership from rubber hand to VR to full body; questionnaires are the common measure, drift/temperature/threat responses tend to correlate [E:S7]. **Over-claimed**: drift as clean proof [E:S3]; questionnaire-only proof [E:S4]; therapy claims [E:S24,S32]; "consciousness".

## F. Likely judge questions, two-line honest answers
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is it proven? Does it work on everyone? | No: it reproduces a published effect, and people differ widely in how strongly they feel it (small studies, no population figure) [E:S11]. We show per-person numbers and say n=1; our pilot: [X of N], filled in after the runs. |
| Is the flinch ownership or just startle? | One trial cannot tell. We use an impact, which tracks ownership in published work (n=18) [E:S5], compare with ASYNC and, if built, a control stone; skin conductance [E:S6] is the validated measure and is future work. |
| Vibration isn't touch. | Correct, it is a substitute. Vibration has produced ownership ratings and skin-conductance effects with a small, non-significant drift gap (0.9 cm, n=20) [E:S2]; we chose it for portability. |
| What is the latency and how did you measure it? | Per-stroke send-time error and cue-ack round trip are logged; the PRD target is <100 ms touch-to-sight. [Fill from the run; blank until measured.] |
| Why 600 ms? | The illusion is stronger below ~300 ms and weaker beyond [E:S9]; 600 ms (±100 ms jitter) is well outside it. |
| Is EMG on strangers safe? Is it a medical device? | Record-only, no stimulation current, disposable electrodes, battery-only node, consent, stop any time. The BioAmp is not medically certified [E:S34]; we make no medical claim. |
| What is new versus mirror therapy or VR rubber-hand work? | Not the illusion. A portable, open rig (consumer headset + sleeve + EMG/IMU) on the Chetna pipeline with build-and-break staging. No new scientific finding is claimed. |
| Why three questions? Are they validated? | They are RHI-style items (ownership, referral of touch, one control item) [R:Questionnaire.cs], not a validated scale. Standardised VR-embodiment questionnaires have 16-25 items [E:S13]; a real study would use one, plus skin conductance. |
| How accurate is hand tracking? | Quest 2 fingertip error ≈1.1 cm [E:S12] (no Quest 3 figure found). We use pre/post differences and treat drift as secondary; rating and flinch lead. |
| How is this consciousness (Theme 5)? | We do not measure consciousness. We make visible that the felt body is computed from timing and can be broken; who notices that is the open question, not our data. [O] |
| Who is the user, what is the business? | Today researchers and clinician-scientists; the hypothesis is ownership-aware rehab. No patients, no revenue claim; we are looking for a lab or clinic partner. |
| Where does the data go? | Local session files and the hub; `patient_ref` only, no names; deletion on request; DPDP consent/notice standards apply [E:S35]. |
| Why two ESP32s and a power bank? | To keep motor switching noise and mains away from the EMG front end (PRD §8, §11); the ESP32 is 2.4 GHz-only [E:S40], hence a fixed-channel router. |
| Whose art is that hand? Is the repo public? | Third-party models; the rigged hand's licence is unknown [R:HANDOFF]; no public build until cleared, the procedural arm is the fallback. |
| What if it doesn't work on me? | Then that is a valid result and we record it; it is why the demo has a control and shows your own numbers. |

Attack surface to rehearse [I]. A clinician: proxies not validated against skin conductance or a validated scale, n=1, no norms, expectancy and order effects, no patient data, drift near the tracker's noise. A hardware judge: ERM rumble is not touch, UDP over 2.4 GHz in a crowded hall, EMG coupling from the motors on a shared bank, how touch-to-sight latency was measured across two clocks, power-bank auto-off, safety isolation, why not a LRA or a commercial sleeve. Have one sentence ready for each; none of them is fatal if you concede first.
