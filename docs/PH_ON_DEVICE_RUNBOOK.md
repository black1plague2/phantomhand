### Sensor data comes in on UDP 8790
- What the real boards do (firmware 0.5.0; the firmware author's answer of 18:09 on 8 Oct, HANDOFF_FROM_TEAM section C): an ack goes back to the IP and port a command came from; `sensor_data` (Node A) and `sensor_chunk` (Node B) go to the FIXED port 8790 of the last sender's IP. A receiver that listens only on the port it sends from has acks and no IMU and no EMG. [VERIFIED: PH-E2E-HW-run1, "On the wire", 19:27 to 19:33: Node B 70 `sensor_chunk`, Node A 297 `sensor_data` and one ack in 3 s, every sensor datagram from the node's port 8790]
- The game listens on UDP 8790 by default (the Phantom Hand scene; `OPUS_PH_TELEMETRY_PORT` moves it, for a simulator on the same PC). One shared socket per port serves the node transports (`TelemetryHub`): a datagram goes to the transport whose node sent it (exact ip:port, else the only transport on that IP). The Python tools do the same: `run_pipeline.py --telemetry-port` (default 8790 with `--hardware`); `live_plot.py` and `node_probe.py` send from local port 8790 when it is free, so one socket gets the acks and the stream, and from an ephemeral port with a note when it is not. [CODE: UdpHapticTransport.cs, PhantomEndpoints.cs, PhantomHandSceneController.cs, sim/live/phantom_replay.py (TelemetryListener); VERIFIED in the open editor against the two real boards: PH-E2E-HW-run1, EMG 100.0 Hz and IMU 99.6 Hz in the recorded session]
- One receiver per machine: the game in the editor or one tool holds UDP 8790 on a PC, not both at once. [HUMAN]
- No other tool may talk to the boards during a session. The firmware streams to the last sender only (Node A switches on any datagram that parses as JSON, Node B on any datagram, a keepalive counts), so a probe or a tool run from another machine moves the stream to that machine. On 8 Oct the PC sent nothing to the boards until the Mac side posted `NODES FREE` on the link board (`tools/demo/link_board.py`). [VERIFIED: PH-E2E-HW-run1, Setup; HANDOFF_FROM_TEAM section C] [HUMAN]

### The headset's log and files without a cable (TCP 8796)
- From the APK of 8 Oct 22:54 on, the Quest app writes every Unity log line to a file on the headset (`logs/<utc>_<boot>.log` in its data folder, one per app start, the last 10 are kept, errors and exceptions with their stack) and serves that folder and the session folders (`opus_sessions/<id>/`: session.json, events.ndjson, kin and sens chunks) read-only on TCP 8796. No adb, no cable, and no firewall rule on the PC, because the PC connects to the headset. Every 2 s the headset also says where it is with a UDP datagram to port 8796 (broadcast). [CODE: game/Assets/Shell/Runtime/DeviceDiagnostics.cs]
- On the PC: `.venv\Scripts\python.exe tools\demo\quest_collect.py` finds the headset (its datagram, the Wi-Fi address adb reports, or a scan of this PC's /24 networks; `--quest <ip>` names it), copies what is new once a second into `sim/out/quest_logs/<time>/<device>/` and prints the log as it grows; Ctrl+C ends it with a count of warnings, errors and exceptions per log and the sessions it holds. While a headset is on USB with debugging allowed it also saves the whole `adb logcat` to `adb/logcat_<serial>.txt` and, at the end, pulls the app's data folder a second time over the cable. From any machine a browser or curl works as well: `http://<headset>:8796/` is the index, `http://<headset>:8796/f/logs/<name>` a file (`?offset=<n>` gives it from byte n, `/?since=<ms>` lists only what changed after that time of the headset's clock). [CODE: tools/demo/quest_collect.py]
- Every 5 s the log has a `[health]` line: frames per second, the worst frame, how many frames were slow, memory, battery, and the scene's own view of the run (phase, operator app, sleeve, muscle sensor, strokes acked, hands). That line is the operator card for a run that has no operator app.
- Before a run the headset shows a "Ready" card where the results panel appears later: operator app, sleeve and muscle sensor each "found" or "not found yet", how to start (the operator's Start, or both hands pinched for 2 seconds), and in its last line the headset's id, its address and the log port. It goes when the run starts. [CODE: PhantomStandbyCard.cs, PhantomHandSceneController.cs `TickStandby`]
- A headset on its own (no operator app, no boards) plays the whole run: every stroke is logged as not delivered (`reason: no_device`), there are no sens files, the session stays on the headset until a hub is found, and it passes `contracts/validate.py`. [VERIFIED in the open editor: PlayMode `PH_Standalone_NoHubNoNodes_RunsToTheEndAndServesItsFiles`, 14 phases in 199 s, 57 strokes, 114 cues none delivered (112 `no_device`, 2 `late`), 40 kin files, every session file read over HTTP equal to the file on the disk; PH-U-DIAG-run1]

Headset log, session files and hub view of one run then lie in one folder. [VERIFIED on the Quest 3, 8 Oct 23:01 to 23:25: PH-U-DIAG-run1 sections 5 and 6]

## 9. Safety lines that must not be skipped
1. **Node B with electrodes on a person runs from the power bank only**: never a laptop USB port, charger or mains; the bank never charges while a node is attached; flash Node B with the bank cable unplugged and electrodes off. [HUMAN]
2. One board per bank port; never a board from the bank and USB at once; bank on the table, never on the forearm. Two boards on one bank for 30 min is untested, and the team runs separate supplies (HANDOFF_FROM_TEAM.md:50; 03-SPEC.md:37 says so too). [HUMAN]
3. Say before they ask: consent and the right to stop, spoken (signed where electrodes go on); EMG is record-only, no current goes into the body; disposable electrodes, skin prep, ask about skin allergy, no pacemaker or implant, no broken skin. [HUMAN]
4. Forewarn "a stone will fall on the virtual hand" and allow opt-out; ask about seizures before the headset; seated and short, agree a stop word; wipe the headset between people. [HUMAN]
5. Limits in software and firmware (do not bypass from Unity): 200 ms pulses, 250 ms per motor, 4 sends per second, intensity 150 of 255, 2 s watchdog. Sessions carry `patient_ref` only, no names. [CODE: 02-RULES.md section 4]
6. One named safety owner on stage. [HUMAN]
